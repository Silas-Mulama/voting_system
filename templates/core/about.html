Who We Are

Butere Technical Training Institute (Butere TTI) is a leading public institution dedicated to excellence in training and integrity. This platform was developed to empower students through a reliable, digital-first approach to choosing their leadership.

Our Mission

To provide a trusted, accessible, and auditable electronic voting platform that strengthens democratic processes and ensures every student's voice is heard without compromise.

The Election Hierarchy

Our platform mirrors real-world democratic structures to ensure organizational clarity.

01

Election

The overarching event (e.g., "SGC Elections 2026").

02

Position

The specific office being contested (e.g., "President").

03

Candidate

The individual student running for that specific office.

Security & Integrity

Verified Access

Only registered students with active credentials can cast ballots.

Single Vote Policy

Our algorithm prevents duplicate voting across all positions.

Audit Trail

System logs track activity to ensure no tampering occurs during the window.

Voter Anonymity

While we track who voted, your actual ballot remains encrypted and private.

Our Commitment to You

Transparency is not just a feature; it's our foundation. We are constantly evolving based on student feedback and security best practices.
